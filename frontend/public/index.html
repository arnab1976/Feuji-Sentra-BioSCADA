BioSCADA AI — Threshold Monitoring & Agentic Maintenance

An end-to-end, multi-agent AI ecosystem that automates SCADA breach diagnosis, retrieves validated SOP remedies from a governed knowledge base, and executes corrective actions under zero-trust, 21 CFR Part 11 GxP compliance.

Streaming Event-Driven Telemetry & Multi-Agent AI Architecture

High-frequency IoT sensor telemetry is ingested through Kafka/Redpanda and continuously aggregated by Apache Flink SQL. Machine learning models predict parameter excursions in real time, while autonomous parameter agents query structured CAPA records and unstructured SOPs to formulate cited, compliant remedies.
