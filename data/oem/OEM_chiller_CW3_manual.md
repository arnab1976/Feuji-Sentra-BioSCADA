# Section 4.2 — Reduced cooling capacity

Symptom: discharge temperature above 8 degC with the compressor running at full demand.

Probable causes in order of frequency: condenser fouling; low refrigerant charge; failed expansion valve; fouled plate heat exchanger on the process side.

Recommended checks: verify condenser inlet air is unobstructed and inlet temperature is below 35 degC; read refrigerant suction and discharge pressures against the nameplate table; if the plate heat exchanger differential pressure exceeds 35 kPa, clean-in-place is required before further diagnosis.

Maximum permitted coolant setpoint reduction rate is 5 percent per 90 second interval. Exceeding this rate risks thermal shock to the plate pack and will void the warranty on the exchanger assembly.
