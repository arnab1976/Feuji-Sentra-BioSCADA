# Purpose

Defines the response to pH departures from the validated band of 6.8-7.2 during fermentation.

# Critical GxP constraint

All pH setpoint changes require QA electronic signature before execution under 21 CFR Part 11. No autonomous pH setpoint adjustment is permitted by any automated system. An automated agent may prepare and recommend the adjustment but must hold execution pending signature.

# Procedure — pH falling below band

Step 1. Confirm probe calibration age. If the probe was last calibrated more than 14 days ago, or the millivolt offset exceeds 12 mV, treat the reading as suspect and verify with an offline sample before dosing.

Step 2. Stage base dosing pump P-2 at an increment of no more than 3 percent of nominal dose rate. CO2 accumulation is the most frequent driver of downward pH drift in the growth phase.

Step 3. Confirm agitator speed is at setpoint. Poor mixing produces local pH gradients that the probe reads as a bulk excursion.

Step 4. Await QA electronic signature before committing the dose-rate change.

Step 5. Observe for two residence times after the dose change. Do not stack a second adjustment before the first has fully expressed.

# Procedure — pH rising above band

Step 1. Confirm probe calibration as above.

Step 2. Stage acid dosing pump P-3 at an increment of no more than 3 percent.

Step 3. Await QA electronic signature before committing.

# Escalation

Any pH excursion reaching the trip band of 6.4-7.6 requires immediate notification of QA and the Production Lead, and batch disposition review.
