# Purpose

Response to water-for-injection conductivity outside 700-900 uS/cm on loop WFI-02.

# Procedure

Step 1. Cross-check the WFI feed composition against the approved batch recipe. Feed composition error is the leading cause of conductivity excursion and is correctable at source.

Step 2. Read the total organic carbon level. If TOC correlates with the conductivity rise, trigger loop hot-recirculation per the sanitisation method.

Step 3. Review the resin-bed regeneration cycle count. If the count exceeds the validated limit, the resin bed is exhausted and regeneration must be scheduled before the loop returns to service.

Step 4. Adjust the feed valve in increments of no more than 5 percent, allowing full loop turnover between adjustments.

Step 5. Conductivity outside the trip band of 600-1000 uS/cm renders the water non-conforming for injection use. Isolate the loop and quarantine any affected material.
