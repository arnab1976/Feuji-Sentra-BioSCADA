# Purpose

Response to cleanroom relative humidity outside 40-55 percent in CR-A1.

# Procedure

Step 1. Raise or lower the HVAC fan speed setpoint in increments of no more than 6 percent. Fan speed is the fastest-acting control on cleanroom RH.

Step 2. Inspect the cooling-coil temperature. A coil running warm cannot condense sufficient moisture and will not hold the RH band regardless of fan speed.

Step 3. Read HEPA differential pressure. A loaded HEPA restricts airflow and presents as an RH excursion.

Step 4. Review the door-open count for the preceding hour. Repeated door openings produce transient RH spikes that are not equipment faults and should not trigger a maintenance action.

Step 5. Log a time-out-of-spec deviation if RH remains outside band for more than 15 minutes.
