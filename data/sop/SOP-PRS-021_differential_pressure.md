# Purpose

Response to differential pressure excursions outside 100-110 kPa on filtration skid FIL-07.

# Procedure — rising differential pressure

Step 1. Initiate a filter differential pressure integrity check on FIL-07 per the attached test method. Rising dP most commonly indicates progressive filter blockage.

Step 2. Open exhaust vent V-12 in increments of up to 10 percent to relieve headspace pressure while the filter condition is assessed.

Step 3. Compare current dP against the bubble-point threshold recorded at filter installation. If the filter fails bubble point, the filter must be changed and a work order raised before the batch continues.

Step 4. Check the gas exhaust flow. A restricted exhaust presents identically to a blocked filter at the pressure transmitter but requires a different remedy.

Step 5. If dP continues to rise after venting, hold the batch and escalate to Engineering.

# Procedure — falling differential pressure

A falling dP may indicate a seal breach or a bypass. Verify the seal integrity index and inspect for leakage. A sudden dP drop is a containment concern, not merely a process excursion, and requires immediate escalation.
